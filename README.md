# EcoCalculator-Climate-Simulator
EcoCalculator & Climate Simulator (School Edition)
A **simple prototype** designed to help classmates and school friends calculate their daily carbon footprint and simulate long-term climate scenarios in a clear, interactive way.
---
##About The Project
**EcoCalculator & Climate Simulator** is a lightweight tool built to raise environmental awareness among students. It allows users to track their everyday environmental impact—such as school commutes and classroom resource consumption—and see how small daily habits affect the planet over time.
**Note:** This project is a **simple prototype** created specifically for school educational activities and science demonstrations.
---
## School Purpose
* **Environmental Awareness:** Educates students on the fundamentals of sustainability and carbon footprints.
* **Practical Commute Tracking:** Helps friends calculate emissions related to their daily travel to school and study habits.
* **Positive Habits:** Encourages simple green actions like walking, cycling, or reducing single-use materials at school.
---
##  Key Features
**Student Footprint Calculator:** Measures carbon output based on commute choices (bus, car, walking) and school supply usage.
**Simple Climate Simulator:** Visualizes basic temperature trends and environmental impacts over time using custom calculation logic.
**Actionable Eco-Tips:** Provides practical, easy-to-implement advice for students to reduce their footprint during the school day.
---
## Tech Stack

* **C++:** Handles the backend logic, mathematical calculations, and simulation calculations efficiently.
* **HTML5:** Provides the structural markup for the web interface.
* **CSS3:** Delivers responsive styling, clean typography, and UI presentation.
